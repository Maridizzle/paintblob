Privacy & your data

Last updated: SET_DATE_ON_PUBLISH

paintblob is a paint-by-number game. You can play it forever without an account; everything you paint is saved on your own device and nowhere else. This page is about the optional cloud account in Settings, which backs up your progress and lets you pick it up on another device.

If you never sign in

Nothing leaves your device. There are no analytics, no ads, no trackers, and no third-party scripts in the game.

What a cloud account stores

  • Your email address, so you can sign in and so the account is yours. If you sign in with Google, we receive the email address and display name Google shares; nothing else from your Google account, and never your Google password.
  • Your game save: painted pictures, settings, avatar, story progress, items. The last ten versions are kept, so a bad upload can be rolled back.
  • Signed-in devices: a short label like “Android · Chrome” and when each was last used, so you can see and sign out any device from Settings.
  • That you confirmed you are 13 or older, and when.

There are no passwords to store. Sign-in codes and links are single use, expire in ten minutes, and are kept only as one-way hashes.

What it never does

  • No selling, renting or sharing of your data with anyone.
  • No marketing email. The only messages are the sign-in codes you ask for.
  • No profiling, no advertising identifiers, no reading of your save for anything but showing it back to you.

Who handles the data on our behalf

  • Railway hosts the account server and its database.
  • Postmark delivers sign-in emails. Their delivery logs keep the address and message for a short period, set to the minimum they offer.
  • Backblaze B2 stores nightly database backups. Every backup is encrypted before it is uploaded, with a key only the game’s owner holds, and is deleted after about thirty days.
  • Google, only if you choose “Continue with Google”, and only for that sign-in step.

Children

Cloud accounts are for people 13 and older. Making an account requires confirming that. Under 13? Play without an account: the whole game works, and your progress stays on your device. If an account was made by someone under 13, tell us and it will be deleted.

Your rights, built in

  • See everything: Settings › Account › Download my data gives you one file with all of it.
  • Leave any time: Settings › Account › Delete cloud account erases the account, every cloud copy of your save, and every sign-in, immediately and for good. Your device keeps its own save.
  • Sign out anywhere: the devices list lets you sign out one device or all of them.

If you are in the EU or UK, these are the rights of access, portability and erasure under the GDPR, and the buttons above are how you exercise them; no email required.
